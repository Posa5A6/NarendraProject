About Hotel Rajshri

Shruthi Pure Veg. AC Restaurant

Our Story

A pure vegetarian restaurant built around the idea of serving fresh, honest food to everyone who walks through our doors.

Hotel Rajshri operates as Shruthi Pure Veg. AC Restaurant, serving pure vegetarian meals to local residents, travellers, and train passengers in the railway station area.

Additional history, founding details, and milestones will be added here once officially provided by the client.

Pure Vegetarian Food

Every dish is prepared with 100% vegetarian ingredients in a clean, controlled kitchen environment.

We take pride in offering a diverse menu that includes North Indian curries, South Indian favourites, Indo-Chinese snacks, breads, rice, noodles, and desserts — all prepared without any non-vegetarian ingredients.

  • Fresh vegetables and paneer
  • Clean and hygienic preparation
  • Air-conditioned dining space
  • Convenient for travellers and families
